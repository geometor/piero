Piero della Francesca¶

The works of Piero della Francesca (c. 1415–1492), master painter and geometer of Sansepolcro, unified Renaissance perspective, Euclidean stereometry, and regular polyhedra. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, these works are liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.
